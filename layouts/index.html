Pasty is your friendly cloud clipboard. Copy a link or a bit of text on your phone and paste it on your computer, without emailing it to yourself ever again.

One clipboard, every device

Use the Android app or the web app to copy into Pasty, then grab it on the other device. Almost like copy & paste across screens.

Works in any browser

No app at hand? The web app runs anywhere, nothing to install.

Share the good stuff

Found this awesome YouTube video on your phone? Paste the link on the big screen and show your friends.

Completely free

No subscription, no ads. Pasty is a hobby project, and the bill is on me.

Your data stays yours

Signing up asks for surprisingly little, and your data is never sold or mined.

Open API

Want a client for your platform? The REST API is open, so go build it.
